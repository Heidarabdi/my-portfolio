## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ultra-wide homepage hero artwork for Heidar, a full stack developer and machine learning engineer. Create a striking, gallery-quality cinematic 3D still of one monumental sculptural capital H, machined from polished dark chrome and brushed aluminum, with a clear recognizable H silhouette, thick architectural forms, beautifully beveled edges and subtle surface imperfections. The sculpture is on the RIGHT half, three-quarter perspective, large and dramatic, cropped very slightly at the right edge. Its crossbar catches a beautiful cold white highlight; a restrained electric ice-blue rim light describes the outer edges. Matte near-black seamless studio floor and backdrop, sophisticated charcoal shadows, delicate photographic grain, restrained atmospheric depth, physically believable ray-traced reflections. LEFT 48 percent is calm almost-black negative space for white website typography, no objects there; top 12 percent and bottom 15 percent also quiet for navigation and supporting copy. This should feel like an expensive contemporary creative developer portfolio art direction, sculptural and architectural rather than science fiction. 21:10 panoramic composition. NOT a website mockup: only the artwork, absolutely no UI, words, labels, watermark, floating cubes, glowing brains, circuit boards, purple gradients or random particle effects. The H itself is the only letter, a physical sculpture rather than printed typography.
